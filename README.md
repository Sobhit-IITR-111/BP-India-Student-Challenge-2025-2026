# BP-India-Student-Challenge-2025-2026
Participated in a national level Geoscience Competition organized by bp and EAGE jointly.
This branch contains the report of the work progressed in the Phase-I entitled on "Stratigraphically Controlled Channel Reservoir Characterization and Appraisal Planning". 

Project Description:
1) Worked on integrated subsurface analysis to characterize a stratigraphically controlled channel reservoir and support appraisal planning decisions. Utilized seismic attributes (SNA), depth maps, and multi-well correlation to delineate channel fairways, identify structural traps, and develop a consistent geological model.

2) Performed detailed petrophysical evaluation using well log data to estimate key reservoir properties (Vsh, porosity, water saturation, permeability), identify lithology and pay zones, and determine fluid contacts (OWC). Conducted STOIIP estimation with uncertainty considerations to quantify hydrocarbon potential.

3) Further assessed reservoir quality and deliverability through NTG, flow capacity (kh), Reservoir Quality Index (RQI), and rock typing. Based on integrated geological and petrophysical insights, recommended optimal appraisal well locations to improve reservoir understanding and support development strategy.
